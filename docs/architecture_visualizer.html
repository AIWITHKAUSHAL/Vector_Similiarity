AT THE GENERATION BOUNDARY
What leaves the app, and when.
Embedding retrieval
On “Retrieve and compare”, EURI’s gemini-embedding-2-preview model encodes the question and every chunk into 3072-dimensional vectors. Chunk embeddings are cached for the session; scoring and ranking run locally.
Explicit generation
In Streamlit, “Generate with EURI” sends the question and selected context through the OpenAI-compatible client. The prompt asks for chunk citations and acknowledgement of insufficient evidence. Model compliance is not guaranteed.
